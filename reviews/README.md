# Reviews

Review records capture curation reasoning and the human-review boundary. Use `reviews/_template.md` and prefer filenames such as `STG-YYYYMMDD-<slug>-review.md`.

A review record should identify staging evidence considered, curated pages affected, the CREATE/UPDATE/DEFER/REJECT/CONFLICT decision, accepted and rejected claims, relationship decisions, open questions, lint/map results, human reviewer, and final outcome. Claude may prepare the record but cannot approve on behalf of a human.
