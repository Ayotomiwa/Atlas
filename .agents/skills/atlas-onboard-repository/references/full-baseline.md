# Full repository baseline

Onboarding must make curation possible without another broad source scan. Select one immutable source state with `python <ATLAS_ROOT>/scripts/atlas_source_snapshot.py prepare`. A missing explicit revision permits only clean current `HEAD`; dirty or ambiguous state needs an exact commit and may use a detached temporary worktree. Never modify the active checkout. Record the selected/default commits, merge base and relationship; an unmerged branch commit is a knowledge snapshot and its merge base is the future intake anchor. Do not advance `_intake/`. Immediately before preview, clean up only through the helper; cleanup verifies the selected commit and cleanliness, and any mismatch invalidates the analysis.

Assess every lens as `confirmed`, `partial`, `unknown`, `inaccessible` or `not-applicable`, with evidence and stopping reason: source boundary/ownership/domain; build/test/release/publication; source roots; real components and rejected folder candidates; per-component entrypoints/control flow/I/O/dependencies/infra/config/deployment/failures; flows; infrastructure; schemas/compatibility/lineage; operations/runbooks/incidents/standards.

Use two internal phases over the same snapshot. Breadth discovery identifies material roots, candidate and rejected records, exclusions, lens states and a targeted depth plan. Breadth stops when every lens has a state and stopping reason and every material root is inspected, excluded with a reason or selected for depth.

Targeted architecture depth inspects only source routes that can change a proposed record, connection, confidence, coverage or deferral. Every material component candidate needs an architecture capsule covering purpose and boundary, entrypoint or trigger, causal path, dependencies and state, infrastructure, outputs or effects, failure or partial completion, completion signals, exact anchors, coverage limits and stopping reason. When the available source cannot close a material gap, ask in the consolidated clarification round or keep the candidate partial, unknown or deferred. Do not begin another broad scan.

A small boundary may use one analyst. A large boundary may use a coordinator plus independent read-only lens specialists. Reconcile overlaps and contradictions before the preview.

Assess every lens, then stage only independently justified evidence. An infra-only boundary may make `staging.infra` the primary record; create a repository/component discovery record only when it has its own independently evidenced reusable boundary. Do not add an onboarding-specific staging type. Each record needs readable meaning, exact anchors, causality, coverage limits and unresolved decisions. Lead with an architecture capsule for every material component candidate and use tables as supporting evidence. Accept the candidate as curation-ready only when the capsule supports curation without another broad scan, or explicitly keep it partial or deferred with a stopping reason. One preview covers the coherent batch and future maintenance anchor; one approval authorises the unchanged writes.
