# Persistence preview and approval contract

Use one preview and one explicit approval for one unchanged persistence scope.

The preview states what was found; the exact records/files and claims to save or change; evidence/provenance; scope/confidence/unknowns; existing or conflicting knowledge; required user decisions; exclusions; validation; and intake effects. Carry that approval through internal handoffs without asking again. Re-preview only when claims, targets, files, evidence boundary, destructive effects or checkpoint range materially change.

Completion reports lead with: saved/changed, already covered, left unknown, deferred, excluded, validation and next publication step. Stable IDs and internal lifecycle/disposition codes are optional audit detail.
