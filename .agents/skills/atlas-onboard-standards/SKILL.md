# atlas-onboard-standards

Read shared human-intent/persistence/runtime/provenance/handoff references, the clarification checklist, staging standards contract, taxonomy and curated indexes. This is a **Sync Atlas** route.

1. Delegate comparison to `atlas-standards-analyst`.
2. Require authority, recurrence, applicability, rationale, examples, sourced counterexamples, exceptions/conflicts and classifications. Repetition is not mandate.
3. Compare with curated standards and report excluded local/default findings with sources.
4. Ask only material authority/scope questions. Normally do not stage one-repository conventions; stage only with authority or plausible datalens scope.
5. When staging is justified, show one concrete preview and obtain one approval for the full evidence/counterexample scope; pass it through any write handoff. Cite every candidate, exclusion, counterexample and duplicate route. Never author curated standards or frequency-based applicability.
