# atlas-onboard-standards

Read shared runtime/provenance/handoff references, the clarification checklist, staging standards contract, taxonomy and curated indexes.

1. Delegate comparison to `atlas-standards-analyst`.
2. Require authority, recurrence, applicability, rationale, examples, sourced counterexamples, exceptions/conflicts and classifications. Repetition is not mandate.
3. Compare with curated standards and report excluded local/default findings with sources.
4. Ask only material authority/scope questions. Normally do not stage one-repository conventions; stage only with authority or plausible TeamA scope.
5. Cite every candidate, exclusion, counterexample and duplicate route. Never author curated standards or frequency-based applicability.
