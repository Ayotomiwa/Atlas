# Standards discovery clarification checklist

For ambiguous high-value candidates, ask only what establishes scope and authority:

- What evidence makes the rule datalens-wide rather than repository-local?
- Who owns or can review the rule?
- Is the observed behavior merely a tool or generated-template default?
- Where is the rationale documented?
- Which repositories, technologies or situations are included/excluded?
- Which sourced counterexamples, overrides or exceptions are accepted?
- Which controlled standard category and requirement level are supported?

Unconfirmed scope remains unknown. Repetition alone never establishes mandate.
